Welcome! 😃

The time of year has finally come for the Wisconsin State Fair! This year's fair will not disappoint with fun for all ages. Take a break from busy life and come celebrate another year with your fellow Wisconsinites!

Featured Acts

The Circus is coming to town!

The nationally acclaimed traveling circus is coming to town for the state fair this year. The theme of this year's event is "clowns takeover the world", how exciting! As always they will bringing the fun for all ages with a show featuring clowns, elephants, and sea creatures! Prepare to be entertained!

Brewers World Series Tour

The World Champion Milwaukee Brewers will be singing autographs at Victory Plaza! Now is your chance to get a photograph with legendary players of Brewers history. There will also be a merch sale for any of you who haven't got your world series champion gear yet!
